Chunk Name: Education benefits without Form 1098T.
Summary: Taxpayers can claim education benefits even without a Form 1098T if the educational institution is not required to provide one, such as in cases involving nonresident aliens or fully scholarship-funded education. To qualify, the taxpayer must demonstrate enrollment at an eligible institution and substantiate payment of qualified expenses. Form 8917 should be used for this purpose.
