Chunk Name: Tax implications of scholarships, fellowships.
Summary: Scholarships and fellowship grants are generally considered tax-free educational assistance, but they become taxable if they are applied to non-qualified expenses like room and board. If any portion of the grant is used for such expenses, it must be included in the student's gross income for tax purposes. For more details, refer to Pub. 970.
