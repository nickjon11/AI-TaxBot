Chunk Name: Academic periods and prepaid expenses.
Summary: An academic period refers to any quarter, semester, trimester, or similar study duration determined by an educational institution. Qualified education expenses paid in the current year for an academic period starting in the first three months of the following year can be included in the tuition and fees deduction for the current year only.

Chunk Content:
.  Academic Period An academic period is any quarter, semester, trimester, or any  other period of study as reasonably determined by an eligible  educational institution. If an eligible educational institution uses  credit hours or clock hours and doesn’t have academic terms,  each payment period may be treated as an academic period.  Prepaid Expenses Qualified education expenses paid in the current year for an  academic period that begins in the first 3 months of the  following year can be used in figuring the tuition and fees  deduction for the current year only. Example 2. Qualified education expenses paid in 2018 for an  academic period that begins in the first 3 months of 2019 can  be used in figuring the tuition and fees deduction for 2018 only.
