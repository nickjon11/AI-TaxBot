Chunk Name: Tuition deduction eligibility guidelines.
Summary: Qualified tuition payments can only be used for the tax year in which they are made, meaning payments for future or past years cannot be applied to current year deductions. For example, a $2,000 payment made in December 2019 for the 2020 winter quarter can only be deducted for 2019. Additionally, qualified education expenses must be adjusted by any tax-free educational assistance received.
