Chunk Name: Correcting Form W-2 errors.
Summary: If the address on a Form W2 is incorrect, you must either issue a corrected Form W2 marked “REISSUED STATEMENT” or a Form W2c with the correct address, without sending Copy A to the SSA. In cases of duplicate W2s or incorrect wage reporting, you can correct the errors by filing a Form W3c along with a Form W2c, detailing the previously reported and correct amounts.
