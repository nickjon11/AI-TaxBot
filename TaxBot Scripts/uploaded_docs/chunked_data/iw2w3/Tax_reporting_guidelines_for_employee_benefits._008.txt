Chunk Name: Tax reporting guidelines for employee benefits.
Summary: The text outlines various types of payments and contributions that are includable as wages or income for tax purposes, including payments for nonjob-related education, employer-paid employee taxes, designated Roth contributions, and distributions from nonqualified deferred compensation plans. It also mentions specific reporting requirements for statutory employees and health savings accounts. Additionally, it highlights the treatment of certain moving expenses and reimbursements.
