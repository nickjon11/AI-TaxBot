Chunk Name: Employee compensation and tax reporting.
Summary: The text outlines various types of compensation and tax reporting requirements for employees, including payments to former employees on active military duty, other compensation types, and salary reduction contributions to retirement plans. It specifies how to report federal income tax withheld, social security wages, and the treatment of noncash payments and reimbursements. Additionally, it notes the maximum social security wage base for 2025 and the inclusion of elective deferrals in reporting.
