Chunk Name: Roth contributions and wage reporting.
Summary: Report designated Roth contributions under section 401(k), 403(b), or governmental 457(b) plans in box 3, including amounts deferred under section 457(f) or nonqualified plans as social security and Medicare wages. Box 3 also includes signing bonuses, taxable group-term life insurance costs over $50,000, certain health insurance premiums for 2% or more shareholder-employees, and contributions to MSAs or HSAs, excluding those made through a cafeteria plan.
