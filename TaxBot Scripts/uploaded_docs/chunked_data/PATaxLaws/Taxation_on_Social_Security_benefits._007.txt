Chunk Name: Taxation on Social Security benefits.
Summary: Social Security benefits may be subject to federal taxes for individuals aged 65 or older, widows or widowers aged 50 or older, and disabled persons aged 18 or older. Up to 50% of benefits can be taxed for individuals earning $25,000 to $34,000, and 85% for those earning over $34,000; for married couples, the thresholds are $32,000 to $44,000.
