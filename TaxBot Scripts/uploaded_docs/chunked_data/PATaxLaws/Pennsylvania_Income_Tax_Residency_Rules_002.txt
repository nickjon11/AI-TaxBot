Chunk Name: Pennsylvania Income Tax Residency Rules
Summary: Individuals must file a PA Income Tax Return (PA40) if they earn $1 or more, with residency status determining tax obligations. PA residents are those domiciled in PA or who spend over 183 days in the state, while nonresidents spend no more than 30 days in PA. Special considerations apply for military personnel, college students, and minors regarding domicile status.
