Chunk Name: Tax filing rules and statuses.
Summary: A joint tax return cannot be filed without permission from both spouses, and specific filing statuses apply for deceased individuals and those who qualify as Head of Household. There are eight classes of income, including compensation, interest, dividends, and gambling winnings. Tax returns are due by April 15, 2025, with a possible extension to October 15, 2025, and penalties accrue for late filings.
