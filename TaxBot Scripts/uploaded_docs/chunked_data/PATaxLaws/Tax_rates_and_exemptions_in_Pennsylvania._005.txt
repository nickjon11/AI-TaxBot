Chunk Name: Tax rates and exemptions in Pennsylvania.
Summary: Pennsylvania imposes a 5% penalty on unpaid taxes, with a maximum of 25% for late payments, unless there is a reasonable cause. The state has a flat individual income tax rate of 3.07%, which is 3.75% for Philadelphia residents, and a corporate income tax rate of 7.99%. Property tax is generally 1.26% of assessed home value, and there are tax breaks for residents aged 65 and older through the Property Tax/R
