Chunk Name: Education expense reporting process.
Summary: Complete a separate worksheet for each student for each academic period in 2024 for which qualified education expenses were paid. Calculate total qualified education expenses, then subtract adjustments for tax-free educational assistance and refunds to find the adjusted qualified education expenses. If the result is zero or less, enter 0.
