Chunk Name: Tax-free educational assistance options.
Summary: Employer-provided educational assistance, veterans' educational assistance, and other excludable educational assistance are tax-free. Including some or all of a scholarship or fellowship grant in income may increase the value of an education credit. Generally, scholarships and fellowship grants are considered tax-free educational assistance.
