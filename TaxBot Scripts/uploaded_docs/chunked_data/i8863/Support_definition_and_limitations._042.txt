Chunk Name: Support definition and limitations.
Summary: If capital does not generate income and personal services do, the 30% limit on support does not apply. Support includes essentials like food, shelter, and education, measured by expenses or fair market value. Scholarships are not considered support for full-time students in 2024.

Support includes food, shelter, clothing, medical and  dental care, education, and the like. Generally, the amount of an  item of support will be the amount of expenses paid by the one  furnishing such item. If the item of support is in the form of  property or lodging, measure the amount of such item of support  by its fair market value. To figure your support, count support  provided by you, your parents, and others.
