Chunk Name: Tax credit eligibility for education.
Summary: The text outlines eligibility criteria for educational tax credits, highlighting that one credit is nonrefundable and limited to four years of postsecondary education, while the other is available for an unlimited number of years and does not require enrollment in a degree program. Additionally, the first credit requires at least halftime enrollment in 2024, while the second can apply to one or more courses. A felony drug conviction affects eligibility for the credits as of the end of 2024.
