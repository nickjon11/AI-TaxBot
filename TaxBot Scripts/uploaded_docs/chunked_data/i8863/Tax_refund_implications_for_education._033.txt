Chunk Name: Tax refund implications for education.
Summary: Tax-free educational assistance received after 2024 may affect your 2024 income tax return, depending on whether it is received before or after filing. Refunds of qualified education expenses can reduce those expenses for the tax year or require repayment of previously claimed credits. To calculate adjusted qualified education expenses for 2024, add all expenses paid and subtract any refunds received during that year.
