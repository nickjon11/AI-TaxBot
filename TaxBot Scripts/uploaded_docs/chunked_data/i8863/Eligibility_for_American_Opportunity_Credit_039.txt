Chunk Name: Eligibility for American Opportunity Credit
Summary: To qualify for a refundable American opportunity credit, you must be under 24 and over 18 at the end of 2024, a full-time student, have earned income less than half of your support, and have at least one living parent. If you meet these criteria, check the box next to line 7 and proceed accordingly. If not, complete line 8 to determine eligibility.
