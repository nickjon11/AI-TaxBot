Chunk Name: Eligibility for education credits.
Summary: Accredited postsecondary institutions, including some outside the U.S., qualify as eligible educational institutions for student aid programs. For claiming education credits, separate forms must be completed for each individual, and specific instructions are provided for the American Opportunity Credit on Form 8863.
