Chunk Name: Tax-free education funding guidelines.
Summary: Tax-free distributions from a Coverdell ESA or 529 plan can be used for qualified education expenses or certain other expenses without incurring tax liability. An education credit can be claimed in the same year as a tax-free distribution, provided the same expenses are not used for both. Additionally, tax-free educational assistance received after 2024 may be treated as a refund for qualified education expenses paid in 2024.

Chunk Content:
. For details and more examples, see Pub. 970. Unlike a scholarship or fellowship grant, a taxfree  distribution from a Coverdell ESA or qualified tuition  program (section 529 plan) can be applied to either  qualified education expenses or certain other expenses (such as  room and board) without creating a tax liability for the student.  An education credit can be claimed in the same year the  beneficiary takes a taxfree distribution from a Coverdell ESA or  qualified tuition program, as long as the same expenses aren't  used for both benefits. For details, see Pub. 970, chapters 6 and  7. Taxfree educational assistance treated as a refund. Some  taxfree educational assistance received after 2024 may be  treated as a refund of qualified education expenses paid in 2024.  This taxfree educational assistance is any taxfree educational  assistance received by you or anyone else after 2024 for  qualified education expenses paid on behalf of a student in 2024  (or attributable to enrollment at an eligible educational institution  during 2024)
