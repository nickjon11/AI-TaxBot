Chunk Name: Education credit eligibility and dependencies.
Summary: Only the person who claims a student as a dependent can claim an education credit for that student. If the student is not claimed as a dependent, they can claim the credit themselves. Additionally, qualified education expenses paid by a third party are considered as paid by the student, unless the student is a dependent, in which case the expenses are treated as paid by the person claiming them.
