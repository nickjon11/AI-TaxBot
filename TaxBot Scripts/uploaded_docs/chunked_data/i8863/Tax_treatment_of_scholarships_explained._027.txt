Chunk Name: Tax treatment of scholarships explained.
Summary: A scholarship or fellowship grant is not considered tax-free educational assistance if it is included in the student's gross income and is applied to non-qualified education expenses, such as room and board. Additionally, students cannot include certain grants from Indian tribal governments in their income if they are excluded under the Tribal General Welfare Exclusion Act of 2014. Coordination with other financial aid, like Pell grants, is also relevant.
