Chunk Name: Pell Grant tax implications explained.
Summary: If you apply the Pell grant to qualified education expenses, it is considered a tax-free scholarship, and your child won't include it in gross income. If the entire grant is used for education expenses, there will be no adjusted qualified education expenses left for tax credits. However, if only part of the grant is applied to education expenses and the rest to room and board, the portion for room and board will be included in gross income.
