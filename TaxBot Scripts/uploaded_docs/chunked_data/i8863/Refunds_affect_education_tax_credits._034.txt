Chunk Name: Refunds affect education tax credits.
Summary: Refunds of qualified education expenses received after 2024 must be accounted for in your 2024 income tax return. If the refund is received before filing, reduce the qualified expenses by the refund amount; if received after filing, you may need to repay some or all of the claimed credit. This process is referred to as credit recapture.

Chunk Content:
. Refunds received after 2024, but before your income tax  return is filed. If anyone receives a refund after 2024 of  TIP qualified education expenses paid on behalf of a student in 2024  and the refund is received before you file your 2024 income tax  return, reduce the amount of qualified education expenses for  2024 by the amount of the refund. Refunds received after 2024 and after your income tax  return is filed. If anyone receives a refund after 2024 of  qualified education expenses paid on behalf of a student in 2024  and the refund is received after you file your 2024 income tax  return, you may need to repay some or all of the credit that you  claimed.
