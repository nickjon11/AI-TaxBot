Chunk Name: Education credits eligibility and requirements.
Summary: The 2024 Instructions for Form 8863 outline eligibility for the American Opportunity and Lifetime Learning Credits, with modified adjusted gross income limits set at $180,000 for married couples filing jointly and $90,000 for single filers. To claim these credits, taxpayers must receive Form 1098T, Tuition Statement, from an eligible educational institution. For updates on Form 8863, refer to IRS.gov/Form8863.
