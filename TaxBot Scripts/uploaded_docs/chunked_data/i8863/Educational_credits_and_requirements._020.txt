Chunk Name: Educational credits and requirements.
Summary: To claim the American Opportunity Credit, you must have received Form 1098-T and provide the educational institution's EIN on Form 8863; otherwise, you cannot claim this credit. Instead, you may qualify for the Lifetime Learning Credit, which is 20% of adjusted qualified education expenses up to $10,000, allowing a maximum credit of $2,000 per return.
