Chunk Name: Tax credits and eligibility requirements.
Summary: Enter your Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) on line 3, which is typically the amount on Form 1040 or 1040SR, but may require adjustments if certain forms are filed or income is excluded. If you are under age 24 at the end of 2024 and meet specific conditions, you cannot claim the American opportunity credit as a refundable credit, only as a nonrefundable credit.

Chunk Content:
