Chunk Name: Eligibility for education tax credits.
Summary: You may claim an education credit if you, your spouse, or a dependent was enrolled at an eligible educational institution in 2024. The credits are based on qualified education expenses paid for academic periods starting in 2024 or the first three months of 2025. If a student is a dependent, their education expenses are considered paid by the person claiming them on their tax return.

Chunk Content:
. Who Can Claim an Education Credit You may be able to claim an education credit if you, your spouse,  or a dependent you claim on your tax return was a student  enrolled at or attending an eligible educational institution. For  2024, the credits are based on the amount of adjusted qualified  education expenses paid for the student in 2024 for academic  periods beginning in 2024 or beginning in the first 3 months of  2025. Academic period. An academic period is any quarter,  semester, trimester, or any other period of study as reasonably  determined by an eligible educational institution. If an eligible  educational institution uses credit hours or clock hours and  doesn't have academic terms, each payment period may be  treated as an academic period. For details, see Academic period  in chapters 2 and 3 of Pub. 970. Who can claim a dependent's expenses. If a student is  claimed as a dependent on another person's tax return, all  qualified education expenses of the student are treated as  having been paid by that person
