Chunk Name: Claiming educational tax credit rules.
Summary: Enter the foreign address of the educational institution without abbreviating the country name, and follow local postal code practices. If claiming the American opportunity credit for the student, provide the institution's EIN on line 22(4) and note that the credit cannot be claimed for 2024 if it has been claimed for four prior tax years. Check "Yes" or "No" on line 23 based on the number of prior claims.

Chunk Content:
. CAUTION ! If the educational institution has a foreign address, enter the  foreign address here and don't abbreviate the country name.  Follow the country's practice for entering the postal code and  name of the province, county, or state. The educational institution's EIN must be provided on  line 22(4) if the American opportunity credit is claimed for this  student. Line 23 If the American opportunity credit has been claimed for this  student for any 4 tax years before 2024, the American  opportunity credit cannot be claimed for this student for 2024.  Check “Yes” and go to line 31. If the American opportunity credit has been claimed for this  student for 3 or fewer prior tax years, check “No.” See Student  qualifications, earlier
