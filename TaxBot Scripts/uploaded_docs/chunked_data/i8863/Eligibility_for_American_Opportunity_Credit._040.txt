Chunk Name: Eligibility for American Opportunity Credit.
Summary: To qualify for part of the American opportunity credit as a refundable credit, you must first determine if you were 18 at the end of 2024 and if your earned income was less than half of your support for that year. If either of your parents were not alive at the end of 2024, or if you are filing a joint return, you may qualify. Additionally, the educational institution's EIN must be provided when claiming the credit.
