Chunk Name: Education tax credits eligibility limits.
Summary: The American Opportunity Credit offers up to $2,500 per eligible student, while the Lifetime Learning Credit provides up to $2,000 per tax return. Both credits have a modified adjusted gross income limit of $180,000 for married couples filing jointly and $90,000 for single filers, heads of household, or qualifying surviving spouses.
