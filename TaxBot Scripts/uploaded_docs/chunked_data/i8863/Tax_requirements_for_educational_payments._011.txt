Chunk Name: Tax requirements for educational payments.
Summary: Payments made in 2024 for academic periods starting in 2024 or the first three months of 2025 require a Tax Identification Number (TIN) by the due date of the 2024 return. Filers must include the educational institution's Employer Identification Number (EIN) on Form 8863, while students do not need to provide the EIN.
