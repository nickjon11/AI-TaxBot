Chunk Name: Maximizing education tax credits.
Summary: Payments made in cash, check, credit or debit card, or borrowed funds can be applied to qualified education expenses without reducing the amount available for education credits. This generally results in a larger education credit, which can reduce taxes or increase refunds. For example, a student with $5,000 in qualified expenses and a $5,000 Pell grant, along with a student loan and additional payment, can still maximize their education credit.
