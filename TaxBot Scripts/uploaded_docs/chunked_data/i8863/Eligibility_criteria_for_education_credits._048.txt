Chunk Name: Eligibility criteria for education credits.
Summary: A student is considered to have completed 4 years of postsecondary education before 2024 if they received 4 years of academic credit for coursework completed prior to that year, excluding credits from proficiency exams. If the student has a felony conviction for drug possession or distribution before the end of 2024, they are ineligible for the American opportunity credit. Additionally, the American opportunity credit and the lifetime learning credit cannot be claimed for the same student in the same year.
