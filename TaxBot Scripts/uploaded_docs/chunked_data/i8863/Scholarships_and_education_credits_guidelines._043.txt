Chunk Name: Scholarships and education credits guidelines.
Summary: To qualify as a full-time student for scholarship support in 2024, you must have been enrolled full-time for any part of five calendar months at an eligible institution or in a qualifying training course. When claiming education credits, enter the specified amounts from the Credit Limit Worksheet and combine totals for multiple students as needed. Your Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) is based on line 11 of your Form 1040 or 1040SR.
