Chunk Name: Student eligibility for education credits.
Summary: Check "Yes" if the student enrolled at least halftime in a qualifying program at an eligible institution in 2024; otherwise, check "No." If "Yes," proceed to line 25; if "No," the student is ineligible for the American opportunity credit. Additionally, check "Yes" on line 25 if the student completed their first four years of postsecondary education before 2024.
