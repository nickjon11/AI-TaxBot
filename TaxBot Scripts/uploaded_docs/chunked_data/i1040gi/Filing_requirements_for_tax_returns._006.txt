Chunk Name: Filing requirements for tax returns.
Summary: You must file a tax return for 2024 if your gross income exceeds certain thresholds, owe special taxes, received specific distributions, had self-employment earnings of at least $400, or received advance premium tax credits. Additional conditions include wages from certain church organizations and vehicle purchase credits. For more information, visit IRS.gov.
