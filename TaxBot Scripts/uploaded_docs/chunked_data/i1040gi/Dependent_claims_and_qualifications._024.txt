Chunk Name: Dependent claims and qualifications.
Summary: To determine if you can claim someone as a dependent on your 2024 tax return, assess if they can be claimed by someone else and if they meet specific criteria, such as having a valid SSN or ITIN and being a U.S. citizen or resident alien. If eligible, complete the necessary sections on Form 1040 or 1040SR. For adopted children, an ATIN may be required if their SSN is unknown.
