Chunk Name: Tax filing extensions and delivery services.
Summary: U.S. citizens or resident aliens may qualify for an automatic 2-month extension to file their tax return if they live or are on duty outside the U.S. and Puerto Rico, but interest will accrue on any unpaid tax. An additional 4-month extension can be obtained by filing Form 4868 by June 16, 2025, although this does not extend the time to pay taxes owed. Tax returns can be mailed using designated private delivery services to meet timely filing requirements.
