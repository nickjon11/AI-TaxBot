Chunk Name: Tax implications for dependents and assets.
Summary: You can file a joint return for the year your spouse died, but not as a qualifying surviving spouse if they died in 2024. An adopted child is treated as your own for tax purposes, and temporary absences do not affect the determination of whether a child lived with you. Digital assets, including cryptocurrencies and NFTs, are recognized as assets for federal income tax purposes.
