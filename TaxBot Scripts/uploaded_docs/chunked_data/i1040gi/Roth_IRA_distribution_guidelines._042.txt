Chunk Name: Roth IRA distribution guidelines.
Summary: If you made nondeductible contributions to IRAs for 2024, refer to IRS Publications 590A and 590B. Specific distribution codes on Form 1099R indicate whether you need to report certain distributions, including conversions and excess contributions. Qualified charitable distributions (QCDs) have special reporting rules and limits, including a maximum of $105,000 per year.
