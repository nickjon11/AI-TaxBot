Chunk Name: Tax dependency and qualifying child criteria.
Summary: To claim a child as a dependent for 2024, the child must be a U.S. citizen or resident, not married, and must have lived with you for more than half of the year. If you are filing a joint return, you can claim the child as a dependent; otherwise, you must determine if you can be claimed as a dependent by someone else. If you meet the criteria, you can complete the necessary sections on Form 1040 or 1040SR.
