Chunk Name: Dividend holding period rules.
Summary: Dividends received on stock held for less than 61 days during a specified period are generally not considered qualified dividends. The ex-dividend date marks when a purchaser is no longer entitled to the next dividend payment, and specific rules apply regarding the counting of holding days, particularly concerning diminished risk of loss. Additionally, certain dividends, such as those from surrogate foreign corporations or payments in lieu of dividends, may not qualify under these rules.
