Chunk Name: Tax reporting for insurance and IRAs.
Summary: Dividends on insurance policies are considered a partial return of premiums and should only be reported as income if they exceed the total net premiums paid. For IRA distributions, report the total amount from Form 1099R on lines 4a and 4b of your tax return, noting any rollovers or exceptions as specified. Rollover distributions must generally be completed within 60 days, and specific instructions apply for reporting nondeductible contributions.
