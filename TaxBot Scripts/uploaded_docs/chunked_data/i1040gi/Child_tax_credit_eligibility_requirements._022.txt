Chunk Name: Child tax credit eligibility requirements.
Summary: To claim the child tax credit or the credit for other dependents, the child must have an SSN, ITIN, or ATIN issued by the due date of your return, be a U.S. citizen or resident, and be under age 17 at the end of 2024. If the child has a valid SSN for employment issued before the due date, you can claim the child tax credit; otherwise, you can only claim the credit for other dependents. For more information
