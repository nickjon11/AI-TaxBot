Chunk Name: Tax obligations in bankruptcy cases.
Summary: Income from code owned or acquired during a bankruptcy case is taxable to the estate and should not be reported on your individual tax return, except for self-employment tax calculations. Wages and compensation must be allocated between you and the bankruptcy estate, with a statement explaining this allocation included in your tax filings. In community property states, income must be divided according to state law, and registered domestic partners in certain states must report half of the combined community income.
