Summary: Report the net amount of tax-exempt OID on line 2a, considering any acquisition premium, and include exempt-interest dividends from mutual funds shown in box 12 of Form 1099DIV. For taxable interest, enter the total on line 2b, and attach Schedule B if it exceeds $1,500.
