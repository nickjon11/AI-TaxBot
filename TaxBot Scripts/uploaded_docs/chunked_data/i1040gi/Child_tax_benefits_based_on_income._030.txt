Chunk Name: Child tax benefits based on income.
Summary: If a child lives equally with both parents, the IRS will consider the parent with the higher adjusted gross income (AGI) as the one who can claim the child as a qualifying child for tax benefits in 2024. If no parent claims the child, the child is treated as the qualifying child of the person with the highest AGI, provided it exceeds that of any parent eligible to claim the child. Additionally, each dependent's Social Security number (SSN) must match their social security card
