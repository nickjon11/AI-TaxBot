Chunk Name: Determining qualifying relatives for dependents.
Summary: To claim someone as a qualifying relative for tax purposes, they must be a specific relative or member of your household, have gross income under $5,050, and you must provide over half of their support. Additionally, the person must be a U.S. citizen, national, resident alien, or a resident of Canada or Mexico. If they are married and you are filing jointly, you can claim them as a dependent.
