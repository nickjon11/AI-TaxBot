Chunk Name: Minimum IRA distribution requirements.
Summary: You must begin receiving a minimum distribution from your traditional IRA by April 1 of the year after you turn 73, or face additional taxes. Pension and annuity payments are reported on Form 1099R, and certain payments, like disability pensions and corrective distributions, should be reported differently. Payments are fully taxable if you did not contribute to the pension or annuity or if you have already recovered your contributions tax-free.
