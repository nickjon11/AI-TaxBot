Chunk Name: Adoption and residency requirements.
Summary: If you are a U.S. citizen or national and your adopted child lived with you all year, they qualify as a U.S. citizen for tax purposes. Certain exceptions apply for gross income tests and time lived with you, including temporary absences for special circumstances. Additionally, if a qualifying child was born or died in 2024 or was adopted in 2024, they are considered to have lived with you for more than half of that year under specific conditions.
