Chunk Name: Tax treatment of charitable distributions.
Summary: When making a one-time Qualified Charitable Distribution (QCD) to a Simplified Employee Pension (SIE), you must attach a statement to your tax return and cannot claim a charitable deduction for any QCD not included in your income. For Health Savings Account (HSA) funding distributions (HFD), you can elect to exclude the distribution from income, but it may affect your HSA contribution limits and requires specific reporting if eligibility is not maintained. If you fail to maintain eligibility for an HSA for the 12 months following the month of the HFD, you may have to report the HFD as income and pay an additional tax. See Form 8889, Part III.
