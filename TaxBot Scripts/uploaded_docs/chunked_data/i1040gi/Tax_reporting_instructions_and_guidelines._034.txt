Chunk Name: Tax reporting instructions and guidelines.
Summary: When entering amounts on Forms 1040 and 1040SR, include cents in calculations but only round the total. Report total wages from Form W2 on line 1a, including spouse's income for joint returns, and exclude certain wages and pension amounts as specified. Additionally, report household employee wages not on Form W2 and unreported tip income on lines 1b and 1c, respectively.
