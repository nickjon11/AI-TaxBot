Chunk Name: Dependent filing requirements and thresholds.
Summary: Dependents must file a tax return if their unearned or earned income exceeds specific thresholds based on age and blindness status. For single dependents under 65, filing is required if unearned income exceeds $1,300 or earned income exceeds $14,600, while for married dependents, the thresholds are similar with additional conditions related to filing status. Higher thresholds apply for dependents who are 65 or older or blind.
