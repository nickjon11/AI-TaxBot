Chunk Name: Amending tax return contributions guidance.
Summary: Refer to Pub. 526 for definitions of cash contributions and qualifying organizations for your amended return. If you have a net qualified disaster loss and choose to increase your standard deduction, use Schedule A for calculations. Line 4a is reserved for future use, and instructions for the Qualified Business Income Deduction can be found in the relevant year’s instructions.

Chunk Content:
. See Pub. 526 (for the year of the return you are amending) for the definition of what qualifies as a cash contribution and for information on the types of organizations that qualify. Also see Pub. 526 (for the year of the return you are amending) for information on whether this deduction is available on your amended return. If you had a net qualified disaster loss and you elect to increase your standard deduction by the amount of your net qualified disaster loss, use Schedule A to figure your standard deduction. Qualified disaster loss refers to losses arising from certain disasters. For more information, see the Instructions for Form 4684 and the Instructions for Schedule A filed with your original return for the tax year of the return you are amending. Line 4a—Reserved for Future Use This line is reserved for future use. Do not enter an amount on this line.
