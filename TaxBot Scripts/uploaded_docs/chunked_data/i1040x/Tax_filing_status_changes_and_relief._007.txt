Chunk Name: Tax filing status changes and relief.
Summary: If one spouse fails to report the correct tax, both may be liable for additional taxes, but innocent spouse relief may be available. To amend a tax return when changing from separate to joint filing status, indicate "Changing the filing status" in Part II of Form 1040X. For head of household status, include the name of a qualifying child who is not a dependent in the designated area.
