Chunk Name: SSN requirements for tax credits.
Summary: To claim the Earned Income Credit (EIC) on an amended return, both the taxpayer and any qualifying child must have a Social Security Number (SSN by the original return's due date. Refundable credits can provide a refund exceeding total tax, and taxpayers should report these credits from various forms on Line 15 of the amended return.

Chunk Content:
. SSN required. If you didn't have an SSN on or before the due date of your return for the tax year being amended (including extensions), you can't claim the EIC on your amended return. Also, if a child didn't have an SSN on or before the due date of your return for the tax year being amended (including extensions), you can't count that child as a qualifying child in figuring the amount of the EIC on your amended return. Line 15—Refundable Credits A refundable credit can give you a refund for any part of a credit that is more than your total tax.
