Chunk Name: Overpayment application and dependents.
Summary: Enter any desired overpayment from line 21 to be applied to next year's estimated tax on line 23, noting that no interest will be paid on this amount. If you owe past-due federal or state debts, your overpayment may be used to settle those debts, and you will receive a notice detailing the application. Lines 24, 26, 28, and 29 are reserved for future use, while line 30 is for listing all dependents claimed on the amended return.

Chunk Content:
. Line 23—Overpayment Applied to Estimated Tax Enter on line 23 the amount, if any, from line 21 you want applied to your estimated tax for next year. Also enter that tax year in the space provided. No interest will be paid on this amount. If you owe pastdue federal or state debts, all or part of your overpayment will be used to pay the pastdue amounts. You will receive a notice if any of your overpayment has been applied to pastdue amounts, so you will know how much was applied to your estimated tax. You can’t change your election to apply part or all of the overpayment on line 21 to next year's estimated tax.
