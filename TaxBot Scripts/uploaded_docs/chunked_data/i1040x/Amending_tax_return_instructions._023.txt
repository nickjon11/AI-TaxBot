Chunk Name: Amending tax return instructions.
Summary: On Line 10 of Form 1040X, report the total other taxes from Schedule 2 for the year being amended. For Line 12, enter any federal income tax withheld and excess social security or tier 1 RRTA tax withheld, attaching any corrected Forms W-2 or 1099-R if applicable. Line 13 requires you to enter the estimated tax payments claimed on your original return.
