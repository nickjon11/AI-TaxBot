Chunk Name: Tax refund and payment instructions.
Summary: Enter the amount you owe on line 20, and refer to the tax return instructions or IRS.gov/Payments for payment options. If you applied an overpayment to next year's estimated tax, you cannot reverse that decision on an amended return. Refunds for amended returns will be sent separately, cannot be directly deposited, and may be applied to any past-due debts.

Chunk Content:
. Enter the result on line 20. Line 20—Amount You Owe See the Amount you owe section in your income tax return instructions or go to IRS.gov/Payments for payment options. If you elected to apply any part of an overpayment on your original return to your next year's estimated tax, you can’t reverse that election on your amended return. Line 22—Overpayment Received as Refund The refund amount on line 22 will be sent separately from any refund you claimed on your original return (see Line 18, earlier). We will figure any interest and include it in your refund. You will receive a check for any refund due to you. A refund on a paperfiled amended return can’t be deposited directly to your bank account. If you owe pastdue federal or state debts, all or part of your overpayment will be used to pay the pastdue amounts. You will receive a notice if any of your overpayment has been applied to pastdue amounts
