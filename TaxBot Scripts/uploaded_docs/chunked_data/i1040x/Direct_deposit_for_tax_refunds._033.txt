Chunk Name: Direct deposit for tax refunds.
Summary: Beginning in February 2023, taxpayers filing Form 1040X electronically for tax year 2021 or later can request direct deposit of their refund into a personal checking or savings account. To do this, complete lines 31-33 on Form 1040X for a single account or use Form 8888 to split the deposit into multiple accounts. The account must be in the taxpayer's name, and deposits cannot be made to accounts not owned by the taxpayer.

Part III—Direct Deposit (for EFiled Form 1040X Only) Beginning in February 2023, if you electronically file a Form 1040X for tax year 2021 or later, you may request your refund be directly deposited into your checking or savings account. If you want us to directly deposit the amount shown on line 22 to your checking or savings account at a bank or other financial institution (such as a credit union) in the United States:  Complete lines 31 through 33 on Form 1040X (if you want your refund deposited to only one account); or  Check the box on Form 1040, 1040SR, or 1040NR, line 35a, and attach Form 8888, Allocation of Refund, if you want to split the direct deposit into more than one account. The account must be in your name. Don't request a deposit of your refund to an account, such as your tax return preparer's account, that isn't in your name
