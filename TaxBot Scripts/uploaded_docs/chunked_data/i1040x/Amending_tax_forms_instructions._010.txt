Chunk Name: Amending tax forms instructions.
Summary: To amend your Form 1040X due to changes in information, enter the relevant calendar or fiscal year, your name, address, and SSN or ITIN. If changing from separate to a joint return, list the original amounts in column A and complete the necessary sections, including dependents and an explanation of changes.
