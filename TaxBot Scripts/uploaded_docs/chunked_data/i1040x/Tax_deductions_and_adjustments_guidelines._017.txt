Chunk Name: Tax deductions and adjustments guidelines.
Summary: When amending your tax return, enter the standard deduction amount for your filing status, which may be increased for age or blindness. For 2020 and 2021, if using the standard deduction, you may claim a charitable deduction for certain cash contributions, with specific adjustments required for each year. Refer to the tax return instructions for limits on cash contributions for the year you are amending.

Chunk Content:
. Standard deduction. If you are using the standard deduction, enter the amount for your filing status for the year you are amending. Remember that the standard deduction for all years can be increased for the age and/or blindness of the taxpayer(s). Charitable deduction. If you are taking the standard deduction in 2020 or 2021, you (or you and your spouse if filing jointly) may be able to claim a charitable deduction for certain cash contributions. For amended 2020 returns only, any adjustments to this deduction should be made to your AGI on line 1. For amended 2021 returns, any adjustments to this deduction should be made to your standard deduction amount on line 2. For adjustments to the cash charitable deduction for any year, refer to the tax return instructions for the year of the return you are amending to determine the limits on amounts of cash contributions you can claim for that year.
