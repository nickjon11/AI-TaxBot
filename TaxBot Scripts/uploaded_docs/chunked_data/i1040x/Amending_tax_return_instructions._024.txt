Chunk Name: Amending tax return instructions.
Summary: When amending your tax return using Form 1040C, include the balance due and any prior year's overpayment applied to estimated taxes. Report the Earned Income Credit (EIC) from your original Form 1040 or 1040SR, and attach Schedule EIC if claiming the EIC with a qualifying child. If changes affect the EIC amount, refer to Pub. 596 for guidance and note any nontaxable combat pay elections on Form 1040X.

Chunk Content:
. If you filed Form 1040C, U.S. Departing Alien Income Tax Return, include on this line the amount you paid as the balance due with that return. Also include any of your prior year's overpayment that you elected to apply to estimated tax payments for the year you are amending. Line 14—Earned Income Credit (EIC) Enter the amount you reported on the line for the EIC on Form 1040 or 1040SR for the year you are amending in column A. If you are amending your return to claim the EIC and you have a qualifying child, attach Schedule EIC (Form 1040). If you changed the amount on line 1 or line 5, the amount of any EIC you claimed on your original return may change. For more information regarding the EIC, see Pub. 596 for the year you are amending. If you are amending your EIC based on a nontaxable combat pay election, enter “nontaxable combat pay” and the amount in Part II of Form 1040X. SSN required
