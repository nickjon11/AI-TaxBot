Chunk Name: Amending tax return requirements.
Summary: To amend a return with a retroactive claim for the child tax credit, the child must have a valid SSN issued before the return's due date. For claims regarding other dependents, an SSN, ITIN, or ATIN must be issued by the same deadline. Refer to Schedule 8812 and Pub. 972 for additional guidance on amending 2019 or 2020 returns.

If you are amending a return with a retroactive claim of the child tax credit, you must have an SSN valid for employment issued for the child before the due date for filing the return you are amending, including extensions. If you are amending a return with a retroactive claim of the credit for other dependents, the dependent you claim must have been issued an SSN, ITIN, or adoption taxpayer identification number (ATIN) on or before the due date for filing the return you are amending, including extensions.
