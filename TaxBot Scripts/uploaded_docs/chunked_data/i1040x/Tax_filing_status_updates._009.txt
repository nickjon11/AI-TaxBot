Chunk Name: Tax filing status updates.
Summary: For 2022 and later, the filing status "qualifying widow(er)" is now referred to as "qualifying surviving spouse," but the rules remain unchanged. When completing Form 1040X, gather information about income and deductions, and refer to the specific form instructions for guidance on taxable income and deductible expenses. Prior year forms and instructions can be obtained from IRS.gov or by calling the IRS.

Chunk Content:
. For 2022 and later, the filing status qualifying widow(er) is called qualifying surviving spouse. The rules for the filing status have not changed. The same rules that applied to qualifying widow(er) apply to qualifying surviving spouse.
