Also see Pub. 15A for special rules that may apply to technical service specialists and test proctors and room supervisors. Transit passes and parking for independent contrac tors. Although you cannot provide qualified transportation fringes to independent contractors, the working condition and de minimis fringe rules for transit passes and parking apply to independent contractors. Tokens or farecards that enable an independent contractor to commute on a public transit system (not including privately operated van pools) are CAUTION ! However, if the value of a pass provided in a month is greater than $21, the full value is part of the gross income and must be reported on Form 1099NEC.
