Chunk Name: Legal services payment reporting requirements.
Summary: Payments made to corporations for legal services must be reported on Form 1099NEC or Form 1099MISC, as the exemption for corporate payments does not apply. To report these payments, you must obtain the attorney's TIN using Form W9, and failure to provide it may result in penalties and backup withholding. Payments to independent contractors are generally reported on Form 1099NEC in box 1.

Chunk Content:
. Payments to corporations for legal services. The exemption from reporting payments made to corporations does not apply to payments for legal services. Therefore, you must report attorneys' fees (in box 1 of Form 1099NEC) or gross proceeds (in box 10 of Form 1099MISC) as described earlier to corporations that provide legal services. Taxpayer identification numbers (TINs). To report payments to an attorney on Form 1099NEC, you must obtain the attorney's TIN. You may use Form W9 to obtain the attorney's TIN. An attorney is required to promptly supply its TIN whether it is a corporation or other entity, but the attorney is not required to certify its TIN. If the attorney fails to provide its TIN, the attorney may be subject to a penalty under section 6723 and its regulations, and you must backup withhold on the reportable payments. Independent contractor or employee. Generally, you must report payments to independent contractors on Form 1099NEC in box 1. See the instructions for box 1.
