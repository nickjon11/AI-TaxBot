Chunk Name: Golden parachute payment guidelines.
Summary: The payment, along with others made to the same individual, must have an aggregate present value of at least three times the individual's base amount. For details on golden parachute payment rules and the valuation of stock options, refer to specific IRS regulations and publications. Independent contractors should report total compensation, including golden parachute payments, in box 1 of Form 1099MISC.
