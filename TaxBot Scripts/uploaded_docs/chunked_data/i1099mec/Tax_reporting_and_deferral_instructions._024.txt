Chunk Name: Tax reporting and deferral instructions.
Summary: Box 12 requires reporting of Section 409A deferrals of at least $600 for nonemployees under nonqualified plans, including earnings from current and prior year deferrals. Box 13 is a checkbox for U.S. payers reporting on Form 1099 to comply with FATCA requirements regarding U.S. accounts. Additional details can be found in the referenced IRS regulations and instructions.
