If the machine rental is part of a contract that includes both the use of the machine and the operator, prorate the rental between the rent of the machine (report that in box 1) and the operator's charge (report that on Form 1099NEC in box 1).  Pasture rentals (for example, farmers paying for the use of grazing land). Public housing agencies must report in box 1 rental assistance payments made to owners of housing projects. See Rev. Rul. 8853, 19881 C.B. 384. Coinoperated amusements. If an arrangement between an owner of coinoperated amusements and an owner of a business establishment where the amusements are placed is a lease of the amusements or the amusement space, the owner of the amusements or the owner of the space, whoever makes the payments, must report the lease payments in box 1 of Form 1099MISC if the payments total at least $600. However, if the arrangement is a joint venture, the joint venture must file a Form 1065, U.S. Return of Partnership Income, and provide each partner with the information necessary to report the partner's share of the taxable income
