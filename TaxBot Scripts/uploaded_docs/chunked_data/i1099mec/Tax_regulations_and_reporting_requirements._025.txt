Chunk Name: Tax regulations and reporting requirements.
Summary: The text outlines reporting requirements for foreign financial institutions (FFIs) regarding U.S. accounts under specific regulations, including conditions for checking a box if no reportable payments were made. It also details the reporting of excess golden parachute payments, defined as amounts exceeding the average annual compensation over the last five tax years, and instructions for reporting nonqualified deferred compensation amounts that are includible in income due to noncompliance with section 409A.
