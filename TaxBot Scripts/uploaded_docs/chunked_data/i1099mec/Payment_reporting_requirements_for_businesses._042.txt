Chunk Name: Payment reporting requirements for businesses.
Summary: Payments made to non-employees for services in the course of trade or business must be reported if they total at least $600 during the year. These payments are generally subject to self-employment tax and should be reported in box 1 of Form 1099-NEC, while other non-taxable payments go in box 3 of Form 1099-MISC. Examples include professional service fees to attorneys, accountants, and other service providers.
