Chunk Name: Tax reporting for damages guidelines.
Summary: Termination payments that do not meet specific requirements should be reported in box 1 of Form 1099-NEC. All punitive damages and compensatory damages for nonphysical injuries or sickness must be reported, except for certain exclusions related to physical injuries, medical care for emotional distress, and damages under specific agreements or for capital replacement.
