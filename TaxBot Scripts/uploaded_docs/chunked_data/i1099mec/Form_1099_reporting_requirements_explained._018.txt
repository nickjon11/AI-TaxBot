Chunk Name: Form 1099 reporting requirements explained.
Summary: If no payments were made to an account required to be reported under chapter 4, enter zero in box 3 of Form 1099MISC. If payments were made but are not reportable due to being below the threshold, the account must still be reported with zero in box 3. Additionally, enter any backup withholding in box 4 for individuals who have not provided their TINs.
