Chunk Name: Tax reporting for various payments.
Summary: A bank financing a real estate project must manage payments and file information returns for contractor payments of $600 or more. Payments to Indian tribe members from gaming profits require federal income tax withholding and must be reported on Form 1099MISC. Additionally, state or local sales taxes paid to service providers should also be reported on Form 1099MISC as part of the payments.

Chunk Content:
. For example, a bank that provides financing to a real estate developer for a construction project maintains an account from which it makes payments for services in connection with the project. The bank performs management and oversight functions over the payments and is responsible for filing information returns for payments of $600 or more paid to contractors. For more information, see Regulations section 1.60411(e). Indian gaming profits, payments to tribal members. If you make payments to members of Indian tribes from the net revenues of class II or class III gaming activities conducted or licensed by the tribes, you must withhold federal income tax on such payments. File Form 1099MISC to report the payments and withholding to tribal members. Report the payments in box 3 and the federal income tax withheld in box 4. Pub. 15A contains the necessary Tables for Withholding on Distributions of Indian Gaming Profits to Tribal Members. State or local sales taxes. If state or local sales taxes are imposed on the service provider and you (as the buyer) pay them to the service provider, report them on Form 1099MISC as part of the reportable payment
