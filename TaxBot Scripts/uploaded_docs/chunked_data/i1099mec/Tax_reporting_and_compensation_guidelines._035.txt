Chunk Name: Tax reporting and compensation guidelines.
Summary: Payments from the HFA Hardest Hit Fund and similar programs should be reported on Form 1098MA. Compensation for injuries or wrongful incarceration must be reported according to specific guidelines, while state or local sales taxes paid to service providers are reported on Form 1099NEC if imposed on the provider.
