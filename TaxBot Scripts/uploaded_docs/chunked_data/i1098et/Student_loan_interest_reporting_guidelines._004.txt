Chunk Name: Student loan interest reporting guidelines.
Summary: Form 1098E, Student Loan Interest Statement, must be filed if $600 or more in student loan interest is received from an individual during the year. This requirement applies to financial institutions, governmental units, educational institutions, and other entities involved in the trade or business of receiving such interest. You can file either a separate Form 1098E for each loan or one form for all loans from the same borrower.

Specific Instructions for Form 1098E File Form 1098E, Student Loan Interest Statement, if you  receive student loan interest of $600 or more from an  individual during the year in the course of your trade or  business. The $600 threshold applies to each borrower  regardless of the number of student loans obtained by that  borrower. However, you may file a separate Form 1098E  for each student loan of the borrower, or you may file one  Form 1098E for the interest from all student loans of the  borrower. Who must file. File Form 1098E if you are a financial  institution, governmental unit (or any of its subsidiary  agencies), educational institution, or any other person  who receives student loan interest of $600 or more from  an individual during the year in the course of your trade or  business
