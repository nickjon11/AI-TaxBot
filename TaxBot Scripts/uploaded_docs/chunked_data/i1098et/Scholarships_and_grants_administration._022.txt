Chunk Name: Scholarships and grants administration.
Summary: Payments received from governmental and private entities, such as the Department of Veterans Affairs and nonprofit organizations, can be classified as scholarships or grants. A payment is considered a scholarship or grant if it is designated as such by the payor or the circumstances indicate it. Adjustments to previously reported scholarships or grants must be entered in Box 6.

A Pell Grant is an example of a  grant administered and processed by a college or  university in all cases. Box 6.
