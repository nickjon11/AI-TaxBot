Chunk Name: Student loan interest reporting rules.
Summary: Only the first person receiving interest payments on a loan must file Form 1098E if multiple parties are involved. For a student loan to be reportable in 2024, it must be either a qualified government or educational institution loan or certified by the borrower for qualified education expenses. Interest on revolving accounts, like credit cards, should only be reported if the borrower certifies the funds were used solely for qualified higher education expenses.
