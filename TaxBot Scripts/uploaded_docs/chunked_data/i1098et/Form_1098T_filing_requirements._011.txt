Chunk Name: Form 1098T filing requirements.
Summary: Eligible educational institutions must file Form 1098T to report qualified tuition and related expenses. This includes governmental units and their agencies, as well as any entity collecting payments on behalf of the institution. Insurers making refunds or reimbursements for these expenses are also required to file Form 1098T.

Chunk Content:
. Who must file. You must file Form 1098T if you are an  eligible educational institution. An eligible educational  institution that is a governmental unit, or an agency or  instrumentality of a governmental unit, is subject to the  reporting requirements of Form 1098T. A designated  officer or employee of the governmental entity must satisfy  the reporting requirements of Form 1098T. If another person receives or collects payments of  qualified tuition and related expenses on your behalf, the  other person must file Form 1098T. However, if the other  person does not possess the information necessary to  comply with the reporting requirements of Form 1098T,  then you must satisfy the reporting requirements of Form  1098T. Also, if you are an insurer engaged in a trade or  business of making refunds or reimbursements of  qualified tuition and related expenses, you are required to  file Form 1098T.
