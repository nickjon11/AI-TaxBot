Chunk Name: Reporting requirements for loan interest.
Summary: Do not report interest on mixed-use loans or loans made under qualified employer plans as defined in sections 72(p)(4) and 72(p)(5). If required to file Form 1098E, you must provide a statement to the borrower, either on paper or electronically. For more details on reporting student loan interest and furnishing statements, refer to the relevant regulations and instructions.

Chunk Content:
.  Do not report interest on mixed use loans. Loans under qualified plans.  Do not report interest on  loans made under a qualified employer plan, as defined in  section 72(p)(4), or under a contract purchased under a  qualified employer plan within the meaning of section  72(p)(5). Additional information.  For more information about  reporting student loan interest, see Regulations section  1.6050S3. Statements to borrowers. If you are required to file  Form 1098E, you must provide a statement or acceptable  substitute, on paper or electronically, to the borrower. For  more information about the requirements to furnish a  statement to the borrower, see part M in the current  General Instructions for Certain Information Returns. Electronic delivery of Form 1098E
