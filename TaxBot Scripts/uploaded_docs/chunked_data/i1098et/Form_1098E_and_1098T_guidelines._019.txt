Chunk Name: Form 1098E and 1098T guidelines.
Summary: When filing Forms 1098E and 1098T, include the contact information for a third-party service provider if applicable. Provide a specific telephone number for the institution's administrative office, not the general number. An account number is required if multiple accounts exist for the recipient, and the IRS recommends designating an account number for all filed Forms 1098T.
