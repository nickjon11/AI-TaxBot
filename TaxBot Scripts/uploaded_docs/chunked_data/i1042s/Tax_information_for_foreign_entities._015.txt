Chunk Name: Tax information for foreign entities.
Summary: For chapter 3 purposes, a recipient includes beneficial owners of income, certain qualified intermediaries (QIs), and foreign entities with income effectively connected to U.S. trade or business. Recipients do not include nonqualified intermediaries (NQIs), nonwithholding foreign partnerships, and certain foreign trusts unless the income is effectively connected to U.S. activities.
