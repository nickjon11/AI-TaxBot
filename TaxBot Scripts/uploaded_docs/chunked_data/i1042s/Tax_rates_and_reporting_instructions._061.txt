Chunk Name: Tax rates and reporting instructions.
Summary: The tax rate on dividends paid to corporations in Puerto Rico may be 10%, and for specified federal procurement payments, the withholding rate is 2%. When reporting on Form 1042S, different tax rates for the same recipient require separate forms, and specific instructions apply for various income codes and exemptions. Additionally, designated withholding agents with central withholding agreements must follow particular reporting guidelines.

Chunk Content:
. The tax rate on dividends paid to a corporation created or organized in, or under the law of, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico may be 10%, rather than 30%. See Pub. 515 for more information. In the case of a specified federal procurement payment subject to section 5000C withholding, the correct rate of withholding is 2% or “02.00.” For Form 1042S purposes, report tax withheld under section 5000C in box 3b as if the tax were a chapter 3 tax. Enter the tax rate using the following format: two digits, a decimal point, and two digits (for example, “30.00” for 30%). However, if the income is exempt from tax under a U.S. tax treaty or the Internal Revenue Code, enter “00.00.” If the tax rate is less than 10%, enter a zero before the tax rate (for example, “04.00” for 4%).
