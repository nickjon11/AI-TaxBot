Chunk Name: Reporting financial account information requirements.
Summary: Report the recipient's identifying number and date of birth in the specified format (YYYYMMDD) if applicable. The primary withholding agent's name and EIN must be provided if reporting amounts withheld by another agent. Additionally, include details about any intermediary or flowthrough entity involved, including their status codes and identification numbers.

Box 13l—Recipient’s Date of Birth Use box 13l to enter the recipient’s date of birth. The correct format if entered is YYYYMMDD (for example, enter “20001205” for a date of birth of December 5, 2000). A financial institution making a payment with respect to a financial account (as defined in Regulations section 1.14715(b)) maintained at its U.S. office or U.S. branch must report the recipient's date of birth (if the recipient is an individual) to the extent that such recipient has furnished documentation that provides a date of birth or the recipient's date of birth is identified in any of the withholding agent's files. See Regulations section 1.14411(e)(2)(ii)(B) for more information regarding a withholding agent's obligation to obtain and report a recipient's date of birth.
